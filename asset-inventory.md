Information Asset Inventory – Hopewell Medical Centre

1. Purpose

The purpose of this asset inventory is to identify and document the information assets that support the operations of Hopewell Medical Centre.

Identifying these assets provides a foundation for evaluating information security risks and determining appropriate security controls.

2. Asset Classification Criteria

Assets are classified according to their sensitivity and business importance.

Classification| Description
Critical| Essential assets whose compromise could severely disrupt operations or cause significant harm
High| Sensitive assets requiring strong protection
Medium| Important assets whose compromise could cause moderate disruption
Low| Assets with limited sensitivity or operational impact

4. Asset Protection Requirements

The following security principles should guide the protection of identified assets:

- Confidentiality: Information must only be accessible to authorized individuals.
- Integrity: Information must remain accurate, complete and protected from unauthorized modification.
- Availability: Information and systems must remain accessible when required for legitimate business operations.

5. Preliminary Observations

The inventory identifies electronic health records, staff credentials, network infrastructure and backup storage as critical assets requiring particular attention.

Compromise of these assets could affect patient privacy, service delivery, business continuity and organisational reputation.

6. Assumptions and Limitations

Hopewell Medical Centre is a fictional organisation. The assets, departmental ownership and classifications in this inventory are illustrative assumptions developed for this practical assessment.

Actual asset classifications should be validated through stakeholder interviews, business impact analysis and organisational policies.

7. Conclusion

The asset inventory establishes the foundation for the next phase of the assessment: identifying threats, vulnerabilities and associated information security risks.
